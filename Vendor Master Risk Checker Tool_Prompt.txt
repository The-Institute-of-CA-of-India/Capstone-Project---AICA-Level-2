Observation
A review of 30 vendor master records identified 16 vendors with 22 total exceptions, comprising 11 High, 9 Medium, and 2 Low risk findings. Key high-risk findings involve direct matches between vendor and employee masters: vendor V015 shares a bank account with employee E004, and V022 shares a PAN with employee E007. Furthermore, duplicate entities were identified across PAN, GSTIN, bank accounts, and names involving vendor codes V001, V004, V013, V014, V002, and V024. Additional data integrity issues include a GSTIN to PAN mismatch for V018, invalid GSTIN format for V017, invalid PAN format for V016, missing PAN for V019, missing bank details for V020, and an address match with employee E004 for V026.

Risk and Impact
Overlaps between employee and vendor master data pose severe risks of conflict of interest, unauthorized disbursements, and potential fraud. Duplicate vendor profiles create exposure to duplicate payments and inaccurate financial reporting. Incomplete or mismatched statutory details (PAN and GSTIN) expose the organization to tax compliance failures, penalties, and disallowance of input tax credits.

Likely Root Cause
Weak input controls and missing automated validation rules in the ERP system during vendor onboarding, lack of automated cross-verification between vendor and employee master records, and the absence of periodic database maintenance and de-duplication exercises.

Recommendations
Implement mandatory automated system validations for field formats (PAN, GSTIN, IFSC) and cross-module checks against the employee master during vendor creation. Require strict maker-checker controls and independent verification of statutory tax documents. Perform regular data scrubbing of the vendor database.

Priority Actions for Management
Immediate attention is required for critical vendor codes V015, V022, V004, V013, V001, V014, V018, and V026. Management must immediately freeze disbursements and investigate potential conflicts of interest for vendors V015, V022, and V026. Consolidate or deactivate duplicate records for V001, V004, V013, V014, V002, and V024 after reviewing historical payments. Correct invalid or missing statutory and banking details for V016, V017, V018, V019, and V020 prior to future processing.
