The problem

Manual AP is slow, risky and hard to see

What Indian enterprises and their CA firms face every month

Data entry

Every invoice keyed by hand: GSTIN, tax split, totals. Each one is a chance for error.

Delayed approvals

Paper and email chains with no view of who is holding what, or for how long.

Duplicate payments

The same invoice arrives by email and in hard copy, and can be booked twice.

No visibility

Spend by cost centre and budget variance appear only after month-end work.

MSMED risk

The 45-day limit under section 15 is hard to track without a live view of open MSME invoices.

Data sensitivity

Vendor bank and tax data often should not leave the firm's own network.

The solution

From invoice to payment, on your own network

One system that captures, reads, routes and reports, with a person checking every OCR result.

AI & technology

What makes this intelligent

Practical, explainable methods that run offline, with no external AI service.

OCR intelligence

A neural OCR engine (Tesseract LSTM), plus Indian GST-specific extraction and self-checks.

Pattern matching

Finds invoice numbers, Indian date formats, tax lines and HSN codes. Repairs OCR confusions by position.

Auto-categorisation

Reads the line items and suggests the expense category and TDS section for the accountant to confirm.

Variance commentary

Rule-based narrative naming the vendors and categories behind each overspend.

Duplicate detection

File hash, invoice number with vendor and amount, and fuzzy vendor-name matching.

Confidence scoring

Every field is scored, so people look where it matters most.

Responsible AI

People stay in control

  • Local data. Invoices and OCR stay on your server. Nothing is sent to an AI service.
  • Human in the loop. No OCR value reaches approval or reports until a person has reviewed it.
  • Confidence shown. Green, amber and red tell the reviewer where to look.
  • Audit trail. Original OCR is kept beside corrected values, with who, when and from where.
  • Least privilege. Five roles, client-level access, forced password change, account lockout.
  • Honest limits. TDS and categories are suggestions. Commentary does not guess causes.
