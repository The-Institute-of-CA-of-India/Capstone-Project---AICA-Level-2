AICA Level II Capstone | Revenue Cut-off Testing Studio

1. Extract this entire submission ZIP on 64-bit Windows.
2. Follow Tesseract_Download.txt for scanned PDFs.
3. Open Revenue_Cutoff_Testing_Studio and double-click Start_Revenue_Cutoff_Studio.bat.
   Python is bundled. First launch needs internet access to obtain pip and the app dependencies.
4. Set the cut-off to 31 March 2026. Enable OCR and upload Sample_Data/Sales_Cutoff_Evidence.zip directly.
5. Upload Sample_Data/Dummy_Sales_Register.xlsx. Map the invoice reference, posting date and
   net revenue amount. Customer name and GSTIN are optional. Apply the ledger mapping.
6. Review the results and export the working paper.
