CashSense - how it works

An AI bank and treasury agent for Indian businesses. It reads bank statements, reconciles bank with books, flags fraud and control risks, forecasts cash for 13 weeks and answers questions by text and voice - with the Chartered Accountant approving every change.

1. How data comes in

Two kinds of input are compared: bank statements (what the bank says happened) and the books (what the accounts say happened).

Statement routeWhat the CA doesWhat happens
TelegramSends the file or photos to the CashSense botn8n polls Telegram every 10 seconds and passes the file to CashSense

Commandpython -m cashsense.cli import <file>Direct import (testing)

Accepted formats: a bank's CSV export, a digital PDF, or phone photos of a printed statement.

Books and masters come through an ERP adapter: bank ledgers, vendors and customers (IFSC, account number, credit terms, MSME status), open receivables and payables, commitments (salary, GST, TDS, rent, EMI, capex, FD maturity) and the bank-holiday calendar. The demo uses a mock ERP; Dynamics 365 or Tally can be plugged in without changing the agents.

Questions arrive as typed text or voice notes on Telegram, or through the dashboard's Ask box and microphone.

2. Reading, verifying and categorising

  1. Reading. CSV and digital PDF are read by plain code (exact, no AI). Photos are read by Gemini Vision twice; if the readings differ, a third read decides by majority.
  2. Python proves the reading. On every line, previous balance ± amount must equal the printed balance; the closing balance, period dates, last month's closing, account number, currency and reference format are checked. A statement that fails any check goes to review.
  3. Categorising. Gemini gives every line one of 19 categories, the counterparty from the master, a confidence and a reason. A rule-based categoriser runs alongside; when they disagree the rule is stored and the line goes to the CA with Gemini's view.
  4. Storing. Everything is saved in Supabase (Postgres) with an audit-log entry.

3. The three agents

Reconciler

Matches by cheque number and amount, then amount + date + party. Exact matches are auto-accepted and logged; near-misses (e.g. Rs 1,85,400 in books vs Rs 1,84,500 at the bank) need approval. Leftovers are classified - cheques not presented, deposits in transit, unbooked charges, GST and interest, returned cheques, duplicates, unidentified receipts. Output: a BRS per account that ties to the paisa, with a proposed entry per difference.

Risk Sentinel

Python rules flag vendor bank-detail changes, unknown payees (worse on bank holidays), payments split just below the Rs 5 lakh approval limit, duplicates, unidentified receipts, returned cheques and MSME bills beyond 45 days (sec 43B(h)). For bank changes, an offline browser agent (Playwright + Edge) opens the account-verification page, reads the registered holder and saves a screenshot as evidence.

Forecaster

13 weeks from the as-of date: known flows (uncleared cheques, receivables and payables by due date, commitments) plus new sales and purchases from the same month last year. Four scenarios - base, customers 15 days late, largest customer pays nothing, capex brought forward - with an alert when cash-credit use exceeds 85% of the Rs 2 crore limit.

Assistant (text and voice)

Gemini function calling over eight read-only tools. Voice notes are transcribed by Gemini and answered with a spoken reply (amounts read in crore and lakh). It cannot approve or change anything.

4. Controls: Gemini explains, Python guards, the CA approves

  • Gemini writes explanations, treasury commentary and suggested actions.
  • The entry posted is always Python's template; a different ledger suggested by Gemini is shown, not posted.
  • Gemini may raise a risk's severity, never lower it.
  • Any amount in Gemini's text that is not in the data is flagged; "suspected", never "proven", for fraud.
  • Approval cards on Telegram: Approve / Reject (entries) and Reviewed / Escalate (risks). Only the CA's tap posts an entry (voucher JV/25-26/<card>); every decision is audited with name and time. Only the CA's own Telegram chat is obeyed.
  • Secrets live only in .env; the upload builder scans every file for them before release.
