How it works

1. Extract. Form 16A PDFs are read with fixed rules (certificate no., TAN, period, TDS). If a field can't be read, AI can fill it, but only with values found word-for-word in the PDF.
2. Reconcile. Books vs 26AS vs Form 16A for each deductor (TAN) and quarter. All totals and differences are calculated by rules, never by AI.
3. Explain & draft. AI explains each exception and drafts deductor follow-up emails. Every amount in a draft is checked against the reconciliation.
4. Follow up. Open emails in Outlook/Gmail or download them as Outlook drafts, then track sent / reply / resolved status and reminders.
