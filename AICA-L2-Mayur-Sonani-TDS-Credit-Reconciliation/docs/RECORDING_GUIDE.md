# Recording the capstone video

The course requires a video that shows **your face** and the **technical content**, uploaded to YouTube (Unlisted) or Google Drive (anyone with the link).

## What is ready

- `TDS_Capstone_video_with_script_captions.mp4`: the full technical video (1920×1080, 8.5 min, no sound). The script runs as captions at the bottom left, so it doubles as a teleprompter.
- `TDS_Capstone_video_clean.mp4`: the same video without the script captions (the demo keeps its step captions).
- `TDS_Capstone_script_captions.srt`: the script as a subtitle file, if you'd rather upload captions to YouTube separately.

The bottom-right corner is kept clear for your webcam.

## Record it (about 10 minutes)

**Option A: OBS Studio (free, recommended)**
1. Add a *Media Source* with the video (no audio), full screen.
2. Add a *Video Capture Device* (webcam), crop it to a circle or square of about 360 px, and place it bottom-right.
3. Add *Audio Input Capture* (your mic).
4. Click Start Recording, play the video, and read the captions in your own words.
5. For the opening and closing ~30 seconds, make the webcam full screen (you can switch scenes), as these are "on camera" moments.

**Option B: Zoom or Teams**: start a meeting alone with your camera on, share the screen while playing the video full screen, read the script, and record. The recording shows your face beside the shared screen.

**Option C: Clipchamp (built into Windows) or the Canva video editor**: import the video, record a webcam clip with narration in the app, place it bottom-right, and export at 1080p.

## Tips
- Say your own figures where they help ("for my clients, keying 100 certificates takes about __ hours").
- Keep the sample data on screen. Never show a real client's PAN, TAN or amounts.
- The AI buttons need a model connection (LM Studio offline, or a cloud key). If you connect LM Studio before recording, you can add a short live clip of "AI: explain exceptions".
